SpeedX Wearable — Wiring & Assembly Guide

Per-athlete armband node · LOLIN D32 (ESP32) · 3.3 V logic · generated from the firmware in include/athlete_sensors.h

Heart-rate sensor: the firmware drives a MAX30102 optical PPG module at I²C address 0x57 (SparkFun MAX3010x library, IR LED for beat detection). The common purple GY-MAX30102 / SparkFun breakouts all work. Mount the optical window flat against the skin and keep the strap snug — contact quality matters more than any firmware tweak.

2. Power & safety

  • Battery: a single 18650 or 1S LiPo plugs into the LOLIN D32's JST battery connector. The board charges it over USB and regulates 3.3 V for everything else. Observe polarity — reversed LiPo can vent or catch fire.
  • Sensor power: run MAX30102, MPU-6050, and the GPS from the board's 3V3 pin and GND. The NEO-6M also accepts 5 V (use the board VBUS/5V/USB pin) if its module needs it — check your breakout's silkscreen; many NEO-6M boards have an onboard regulator and prefer 3.3–5 V.
  • Battery sense: the LOLIN D32 already wires the cell to GPIO35 through an onboard 100k/100k divider (BATTERY_DIVIDER 2.0). No external parts. On a plain ESP32 DevKit you must add that divider yourself.
  • Current: the GPS draws the most (~25–35 mA with the antenna searching). The ESP32's 3.3 V regulator handles it, but don't also hang high-draw loads off the same rail.

RX/TX always cross. The ceramic patch antenna must face the sky (out the lid window), not the arm. First fix outdoors can take 30–90 s.

Battery

Plug the 18650/LiPo into the LOLIN D32 JST connector (mind polarity). Charging and 3.3 V regulation are automatic over USB. Voltage is sensed on GPIO35 through the onboard divider.

6. Assembly order

  1. Bench-test first. Flash the simulated build (pio run -e athlete1 -t upload) on a bare board — confirm it joins the mesh before adding sensors.
  2. Solder the I²C pair (HR + IMU): SDA→21, SCL→22, both VCC→3V3, both GND→GND.
  3. Power on, open the serial monitor. The firmware logs MAX30102 optical HR ready and the IMU init. If a device is missing, recheck SDA/SCL and 3.3 V.
  4. Wire the GPS (remember RX↔TX cross). Take it outside; watch for a fix in the logs.
  5. Plug in the battery last. Verify the battery % reads sanely on the dashboard.
  6. Fit everything into the printed enclosure (speedx_armband.scad): HR window to skin, GPS antenna to the lid window, screw the lid.
